## 🏗️ Layer-by-Layer Breakdown

### 1️⃣ MARKET REALITY BAR - The Bloomberg Headline

**Purpose:** Let a PM glance and know "Are we safe and what kind of market is this?"

**Fields:**
- **Regime:** What world we are in
- **Regime Stability:** How fragile it is  
- **Market Pulse:** Is this violent or calm
- **Risk-On %:** Allowed aggression
- **Exposure:** What we are actually risking
- **Liquidity:** Can we get out
- **System Health:** Is the organism healthy

**Implementation:** Real-time status bar with Bloomberg-style metrics

### 2️⃣ BRAIN + BELIEF PANEL - The Cognitive Layer

**Two Columns:**

**Left - Market Brain:**
- Regime map visualization
- Pulse waveform display
- Causal graph network
- Survival alerts system

**Right - Belief Engine:**
- Active beliefs table with strength/evidence
- Clickable belief analysis
- Invalidation conditions
- Conviction gauges

**What Makes It Special:** Every belief is explainable with evidence and invalidation conditions.

### 3️⃣ PORTFOLIO & CAPITAL PANEL - Where Investors Stare

**Three Subpanels:**

**A) Strategy Layer:**
- Strategy weights with conviction levels
- Capital allocator visualization
- Performance attribution

**B) Portfolio Organism:**
- Stock weights and positions
- Sector exposure breakdown
- Turnover and cash metrics

**C) Constraint Monitor:**
- Live green/red status indicators
- Max position limits
- Sector caps
- Turnover constraints
- Liquidity requirements

**Discipline Indicator:** Shows if all constraints are satisfied

### 4️⃣ RISK SPINE - Visually Intimidating

**Big Banner:** RISK STATUS: NORMAL / ELEVATED / LOCKED

**Components:**
- Emergency brake status
- Kill switches monitoring
- Stress indicators with gauges
- Drawdown risk assessment
- Volatility tracking

**The Red Button:** "SIMULATE SHOCK" - Tests system freeze mechanisms

**Purpose:** When investors see this system freeze itself, they trust it.

### 5️⃣ EXECUTION & MEMORY - The Audit Trail

**Two Tabs:**

**Decisions Tab:**
- Chronological decision log
- Time → Action → Reason → Belief → Risk
- Clickable decision analysis
- Approval tracking

**Memory Tab:**
- Similar historical regimes
- Portfolio behavior patterns
- Memory-based predictions
- Historical outcome analysis
