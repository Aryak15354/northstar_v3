## 🏆 The Solution: Five-Layer Narrative Intelligence

### 1️⃣ Five-Layer Causal Reasoning

Every narrative now has five layers (exactly how PMs think):

1. **What is happening** (observation)
2. **Why it is happening** (causation)
3. **What we are doing** (action)
4. **What could go wrong** (risk)
5. **What we will do if it does** (contingency)

### 2️⃣ Narrative Atoms - The Secret Weapon

Enhanced `narrative_atoms.yaml` with causal bricks:

```yaml
liquidity_tightening:
  cause:
    - RBI hiking rates
    - bond yields rising
  effect:
    - growth stocks derate
    - FII outflows accelerate
  market_behavior:
    - volatility rising
    - breadth weakening
  portfolio_action:
    - reduce high beta exposure
    - increase cash allocation
  failure_mode:
    - false tightening signal
    - policy reversal risk
  historical_precedents:
    - 2018_Q4: "RBI tightening led to 12% correction over 6 months"
```

### 3️⃣ Position Justifications

Every capital movement now has fund-grade explanations:

**Instead of:** "Reduced IT sector by 3%"

**Northstar says:** "We reduced IT exposure by 3% because global yields rose 42bps in 6 weeks, which historically compresses long-duration earnings multiples. Our regime memory shows this typically causes 8–15% IT underperformance over 3–6 months. However, we retained TCS due to superior balance sheet resilience."

### 4️⃣ Regime Memory Integration

Every regime change calls historical memory:

"This resembles Q3 2018 and Q1 2022. In both periods, markets initially ignored tightening before correcting ~12%."

### 5️⃣ Counterfactual Analysis (Elite Touch)

Every narrative includes "What would prove us wrong?":

"This view would be invalidated if liquidity improves or earnings reaccelerate."

### 6️⃣ Three-Tier Narrative Hierarchy

**Layer 1 - Market Pulse** (5 lines for daily use)
- Quick consumption by traders and analysts

**Layer 2 - Portfolio Rationale** (1-2 pages for PM level)  
- Investment committee presentations
- Comprehensive reasoning with historical context

**Layer 3 - Regime Thesis** (Deep memory for quarterly reviews)
- Institutional presentations
- Complete historical pattern analysis
